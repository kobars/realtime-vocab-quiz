## PR-137 — Make the outside-the-top-50 fan-out test deterministic

- **Tool:** Claude Code (claude-opus-5-5)
- **Task:** Remove the intermittent failure of `test_players_outside_the_top_50_get_rank_updates`, where `u000` sometimes fell out of the top 50 and received a `rank_update`, without changing any assertion.
- **Prompt / interaction:** The agent got the failing test and the scoring rule, measured the failure rate on `main` (1 of 15 runs), traced it to the points depending on the store clock's millisecond, and changed the test setup so the groups are separated by a whole answer.
- **What was wrong or changed:** The 60 players that should form ranks 1-60 all answered two questions, so each scored 298-300 by chance (150 when the serve and the answer share a millisecond, 149 otherwise), and `u000` ranked below 50 of them in some runs. Giving all 60 a third answer would not have helped: they would still tie by chance among themselves. The fix adds a third question to the test quiz: the top 50 answer three questions and ranks 51-60 answer two, so the top 50 always score more than ranks 51-60, and those more than any single scorer.
